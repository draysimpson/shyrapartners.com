How an engagement runs

Four stages, one team

The partners who scope the work are the ones who do it. You won't be handed off to a junior team after the first meeting.

  1. Stage 1

    Diagnose

    Interviews, data review and site visits to understand what is really happening.

  2. Stage 2

    Decide

    A short list of options, each with its cost, risk and expected return, and a clear recommendation.

  3. Stage 3

    Implement

    We work inside your team to put the plan in place and remove blockers as they appear.

  4. Stage 4

    Review

    We measure results against the targets agreed at the start and hand over ownership.
