Privacy policy

This policy explains how Shyra Partners ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit this website, contact us, or work with us as a client.

Who we are

Shyra Partners is a consulting and advisory firm, and is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. For any question about this policy or your information, email info@shyrapartners.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number, company and the content of your messages when you contact us.
  • Client and engagement information that you or your organisation share with us in the course of our work together.
  • Website information. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Like any website, our hosting provider and font provider receive standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, when your browser requests a page.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and arrange meetings.
  • To deliver our services and manage our relationship with clients.
  • To meet our legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.
  • To keep our systems and information secure.

We use personal information only where we have a lawful reason to do so: to perform a contract with you, to meet a legal obligation, for our legitimate business interests, or with your consent.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, such as email, document storage and hosting providers, under appropriate confidentiality and data protection terms, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including any period required by law, and then delete it securely.

Security and transfers

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. Some of our service providers may store data outside the country where you live. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards required by applicable data protection law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict how we use it, to ask for a copy in a portable format, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email info@shyrapartners.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
