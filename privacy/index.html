Shyra Partners Google Workspace connector

This notice explains how the Shyra Partners Google Workspace connector (the "connector") accesses, uses and stores Google user data. The connector is an internal tool operated by Shyra Partners. It is not offered to the public.

In short
  • Only a small number of named Shyra Partners accounts can sign in.
  • The connector reads and acts on your own Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs and Sheets only when you ask it to.
  • It does not store your emails, files or calendar entries. It stores only encrypted sign-in tokens, on servers in London.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account.

Who we are

The connector is operated by Shyra Partners. For any question about this notice or your data, email info@shyrapartners.com.

What the connector does

The connector lets authorised Shyra Partners staff use an AI assistant with their own Google Workspace account. When a signed-in user makes a request, such as finding an email, reading a document or adding a calendar event, the connector calls the relevant Google API on that user's behalf and returns the result to the user's assistant session.

Who can use it

Sign-in is restricted to three named Shyra Partners accounts. Anyone else who tries to sign in is refused, and no data is accessed for them.

Google data the connector accesses

With your permission, granted on Google's consent screen, the connector can access the following for the signed-in account only:

  • Gmail: to search, read, draft, send and organise your email.
  • Google Drive: to find, read, create and organise your files.
  • Google Calendar: to read and manage your events.
  • Google Docs and Sheets: to read, create and edit your documents and spreadsheets.
  • Basic profile: your name and email address, to identify which account is signed in.

The connector only accesses this data in response to a request you make. It does not scan, index or monitor your account in the background.

What we store, and where

The connector does not store the contents of your emails, files, documents, spreadsheets or calendar events. Data retrieved for a request is passed to your assistant session and is not kept by the connector afterwards.

The only data the connector keeps is the OAuth sign-in tokens that let it act for your account. These tokens are encrypted and stored on servers in London, United Kingdom. They are kept until you revoke access or your account is removed from the connector, at which point they are deleted.

How data is shared

Data the connector retrieves is returned only to the signed-in user's own AI assistant session, where it is processed under that assistant provider's terms. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with any other third party, except where required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The connector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features the user requests. It is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models, and humans do not read it unless the user asks us to for support, it is needed for security purposes, or the law requires it.

Revoking access and deleting your data

You can remove the connector's access at any time from the third-party connections page of your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections. Revoking access makes your stored tokens unusable. To have your tokens deleted from our servers straight away, email info@shyrapartners.com.

Changes to this notice

If we change how the connector handles your data, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
